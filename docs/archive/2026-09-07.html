Zero-shot LLMs benchmarked for weather-related grid outage prediction

This is Con Ed-adjacent research: six years of outage records against high-resolution weather across a Texas utility, testing whether LLMs can classify severity at 3h/6h/12h horizons without labeled training. Supervised models still win on macro-F1, but newer LLMs are closing the gap. Useful ammo when your team weighs zero-shot LLM pilots against traditional ML for storm response and outage triage.
